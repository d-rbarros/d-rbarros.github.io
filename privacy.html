Privacy policy

Claudson casa is used by one household only. It uses Google sign-in solely to send on/off commands for the owner's own home devices to Google Assistant. It stores the owner's sign-in on the owner's own server, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising. This page collects no data.

To withdraw access at any time, remove "Claudson casa" at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
